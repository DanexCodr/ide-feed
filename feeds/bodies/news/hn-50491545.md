On Nov 13, 2026 the FAA ordered a ground stop at every U.S. airport at 3:54 PM ET. This page shows tracked flights over the U.S., live and replayable, against the same time a week earlier.

Rotate your phone for a bigger map

Flight positions: [adsb.fi](https://adsb.fi/) community ADS-B network. Airport delay feed: [FAA NAS Status](https://nasstatus.faa.gov/). An independent, unofficial page, not affiliated with the FAA. Hover or tap a dot to identify the aircraft.

## The shutdown so far

Read from the tracking data, not from official announcements. Times are Eastern.

## Recovery by airport

Departures in the 30 minutes before the selected time, as a share of the same half-hour a week earlier, at the 16 largest airports. An airport counts as back once it holds three-quarters of its usual departures for two readings in a row.

## Departure backlog by airport

Estimated commercial departures missed since 3:30 pm ET: the count for the same hours a week earlier minus this week's, from tracking data. On the right, how many left, out of the number a week earlier. Dot: departures in the last 30 minutes (red almost none, amber reduced, green near normal).
