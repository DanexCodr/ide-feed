Google has acquired Crusoe’s data center site in southeast Wyoming, completing Crusoe’s pre-development work on site selection, governmental approvals, and a hybrid power strategy developed with Tallgrass Energy.

**DENVER — November 6, 2026 — **Crusoe, the AI factory company, today announced its site in southeast Wyoming has been acquired by Google. The transaction marks the completion of Crusoe's engagement in pre-development work on the Wyoming project – which included data center site selection, governmental approvals, and power strategy.

"Crusoe’s pre-construction efforts – origination, entitlement, and de-risking – have set up the Wyoming project for construction and eventual operation,” said Chase Lochmiller, Co-founder and CEO of Crusoe. “Crusoe is in the business of activating gigawatts for intelligence. With more than 6 GW contracted and over 40 GW in our pipeline, we are eager to steward the development and energization of the foundational infrastructure of AI.”

Crusoe and Tallgrass Energy initiated the Wyoming campus through the joint development of power and data center infrastructure. This parallel process required simultaneous coordination across energy systems design, data center engineering, site development, and hyperscaler engagement. The innovative hybrid power strategy developed for the campus exemplifies Crusoe’s integrated approach to digital infrastructure, combining fuel cells, grid power, and natural gas turbines with a long-term roadmap for carbon capture via Tallgrass’s regional CO2 sequestration hub.

Crusoe recently [announced](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-developing-data-center-campus-in-armstrong-county-texas) it is the developer of Google’s data center campus under construction in Armstrong County outside of Amarillo.
