The [NIST board](https://www.nist.gov/el/intelligent-systems-division-73500/robotic-grasping-and-manipulation-assembly/assembly) has twenty assembly tasks, with parts as small as 4 mm. Fitting one part can take several changes of grasp and orientation. Tight clearances leave little room for error: a tilted nut can jam or cross-thread.

With several parts, the robot also has to choose an order. An installed component may block the gripper’s next move.
