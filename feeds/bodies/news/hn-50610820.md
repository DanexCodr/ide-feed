This T&C Pocket Bici is a folding bicycle made by Tresoldi & Casiraghi, an Italian metalworking firm, in 1963. Unlike the folding bicycles of today that split in half, the components here all fold into the racetrack-shaped frame. The whole thing then fits within its own suitcase. (Brompton bike in the photo for scale.)

![](https://s3files.core77.com/blog/images/1855432_81_145766_3dmmTXja3.jpg)

The bike's origin story is a bit murky; one auction site claims it was created specifically for the Chinese market, and that 2,500 units were sold. In contrast, *[BikeFlip](https://www.bikeflip.com/blog/bicycles-that-made-history) *says a Chinese group placed an initial order for 2,000 units, but that after T&C produced the first 1,000 units, payment never materialized and the project was scrapped.
