Google DeepMind robotics researchers have found a short cut to teaching robots new physical skills. Take video footage of a human performing the skill and ask Gemini 4 Argon to build a virtual simulation. Over the years we have seen humanoid robots acquire many different skills. Behind the scenes feats such as throwing a ball through a hoop required hours of calculations, modelling and simulation. These days are behind us— Gemini 4 Argon, Google's frontier AI model designed for complex, deep-work tasks, is endowed with 3D spatial awareness and visual-semantic reasoning that can power "Video-to-Sim-to-Real" pipelines that allow humanoid robots to learn physical skills by watching raw video footage. This "Video-to-Sim-to-Real" pipeline compresses weeks of manual simulation modeling into a few hours, transforming ordinary videos into a scalable, infinite training ground for robotics.  To showcase this capability, Google DeepMind robotics researchers instructed Gemini 4 Argon to watch a video of a human playing cornhole, in which bean bays are tossed through a relatively small hole. This skill requires vision and reasoning and can be honed by learning from mistakes. __ See how Gemini 4 Argon built a virtual simulation in MuJoCo and accurately calculated the physics and whole-body control required for Apptronik’s Apollo 2 humanoid to sink seven consecutive shots for real: VIDEO Not only did Gemini 4 train the robot, it also provided a succinct overview of how it had done it, telling us:

  * _First, I watched a video of a person throwing a beanbag, tracking their arm swing and release to map that human motion onto my own robot joints and hands. Second, I looked through my two eye cameras to measure the exact 3D position of the board and built a physics simulation of my body and the board. Third, I practiced the throw in simulation, widening my stance and tuning my swing so my hand cleanly cleared my hip and launched a smooth arc. And fourth, on the real robot, I watched each bag land and adjusted my release by a few centimeters until we sank seven shots straight in the hole. Woohoo!_
