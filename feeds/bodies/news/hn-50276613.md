I use a double-buffered queue to collect and segment telemetry events into groups bound by UTC hour.
            My pipeline leverages Cloudflare Workers, Queues, Workflows, and R2 to capture, batch, process,
            and store telemetry events while maximizing the benefits of Cloudflare's pricing.

Modulo math is used to route the payload to the active queue and again to drain the inactive queue.
            UTC is used to avoid DST handling.
