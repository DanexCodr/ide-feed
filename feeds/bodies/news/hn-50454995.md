[Gitori's](https://www.gitori.com) [Find the Notes game](https://www.gitori.com/play/FBG-101) challenges users to find a given note in a highlighted area of the fretboard. Over the last 3 years, the game has been played by over 10,000 people[, and I've collected 275,169 different data points on note identification]. Based on that data, I have a heatmap of the hardest notes to identify on the fretboard. Reds are the hardest and greens are the easiest.

1. [[The upper middle of the neck is hardest.] The G string at frets 6 to 9 is the red zone, with the B and D strings at frets 7 to 9 close behind. The single worst spot, the G string at fret 8 (E♭), is answered incorrectly about one-third of the time.]

1. [[The edges are easiest.] The low E string is the easiest string, then the high E. Open strings and the 12th fret are missed about 6% of the time, against roughly 20% around frets 8 and 9.]

1. [[Sharps and flats are harder.] What a shocker! 😜 About 16% of answers on accidentals are wrong, against 12% on natural notes, and even the correct ones take about 11% longer to find (roughly 2.5 seconds against 2.2). F♯, B♭, A♭ and E♭ are the hardest note names; E is the easiest.]
