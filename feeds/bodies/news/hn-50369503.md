I have big news: Conductor is joining Meta.

We launched Conductor a lifetime ago, in the distant past of July 2025. Writing most of your code with agents (let alone a *team* of coding agents) felt crazy back then. You trusted us to come along for the ride, and we cannot thank you enough!

We have some big, crazy plans for the next interface. If you liked Conductor, you’re gonna love what’s coming. We’ll have something new for you to use early next year.

Conductor will keep working as normal till then, except for Conductor Cloud, which will shut down over the next few weeks. We’ve built an easy way to transfer Conductor Cloud workspaces to local, and we’re around to help make the transition as easy as possible.
