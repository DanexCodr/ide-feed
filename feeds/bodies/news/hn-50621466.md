The Power of GraphQL, without ``GraphQL``.    
The last framework that actually makes sense, in the age of AI.

## Why Cobalt?  
It's simple and powerful.

### Why not REST?

#### Endpoint Fatigue & Over-fetching

Managing hundreds of endpoints creates a maintenance nightmare. You fetch huge JSON blobs just to display a username.

#### The Cobalt Solution

One smart endpoint. The client specifies exactly what data it needs. The GraphQL feature everyone loves.

### Why not pure GraphQL?

#### The Double Declaration Problem

You write a TypeScript interface. Then you write a GraphQL Schema. Then you write a Resolver. If they drift, your app crashes.

#### The Cobalt Solution

Single source of truth. Your TypeScript code IS your schema. No client-side codegen steps or schema sync required.

### Why not tRPC?

#### Tight Coupling Trap

Great for monoliths, but your backend logic leaks into your client bundle. Scaling to microservices or exposing a public API later becomes a rewrite.

#### The Cobalt Solution

Loose coupling, magnetic types. The schema is the contract. You can move fast AND scale to complex backends.

### The Cobalt Way

#### Magnetic Type Safety

Define your logic once. The Schema is inferred. The SDK is generated. The types are magnetically bonded across the network boundary.

#### The Cobalt Solution

The best of all worlds. The speed of tRPC, the flexibility of GraphQL, and the simplicity of REST.

### No Boilerplate

#### Stop writing glue code

Traditional frameworks require Controllers, manual serialization, and Service layers just to move data.

#### The Cobalt Solution

Write your logic. Cobalt generates the transport layer. No routers, no adapters, no manual schemas.

### LLM Friendly

#### Context Rot

LLMs struggle with large, scattered codebases. Providing context across 50 files degrades performance and increases cost.

#### The Cobalt Solution

Dense, collocated code. Smaller context window footprint means smarter AI assistance and lower token costs.

### Zero-Config SDK

#### Typed SDKs for free

Writing GraphQL queries manually is tedious and error-prone. Cobalt gives you a fully typed SDK out of the box.

#### The Cobalt Solution

sdk.query.user({ id })() - Autocomplete your entire backend, as if it was local code. No graphql tags, no codegen watchers.
