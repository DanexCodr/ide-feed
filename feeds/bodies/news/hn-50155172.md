					where humans and AI agents work together on analytics.

					governments, often replacing tools like Tableau and Power BI. Our open source project has

## How we work

We’re currently rethinking how we build software, moving toward a development process where
				agents take on most of the software lifecycle. We believe it’s possible to automate almost
				all of this work, freeing us to focus on the parts that require the most judgement,
				creativity, and product thinking.

Our development process will change as AI improves, but our core principles remain the same:

- We take care of our customers. We’re exceptionally responsive and stay close to customers until their problems are solved. We probably take this further than is reasonable, and we like it that way.
- We’re obsessed with product quality. We care about the details and put a huge amount of effort into making our work look beautiful and feel smooth to use.
- We move fast and finish things. We bias toward action and care about getting useful things into users’ hands as soon as we can.
- We avoid meetings. The best work gets done when you have time and space to focus, so we try to make that our default work environment. We have one team meeting per week, and that’s it.
- We’re data-driven. We instrument almost everything, track the metrics that matter, and use data to understand what’s working.
- We keep raising the bar. We’re constantly looking for ways to improve our software development process, and expect everyone on the team to suggest and make improvements when they see them.
