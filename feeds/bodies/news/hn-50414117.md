### ALL-GAMES LEADERBOARD

Every completed game earns XP. Friend matches award 100 for a win, 50 for a draw and 25 for completing a loss. Ampe and board moves are checked by the server; shared arena results are reported by the room host.

Your public nickname and friend-match results appear on the leaderboard. Private rooms expire after one hour. Leaving a live duel ends it without awarding a win.
