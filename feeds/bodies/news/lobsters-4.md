Why should this be a sad day for the C++ community? Taking into account the number of P-members who abstained due to a lack of consensus, which makes the NO vote even more significant, sinking such a flawed contracts proposal is a good sign of a healthy, engaged, and competent community at the national body level.
That some people were willing to put the entire C++26 standard at risk to push through such a controversial and deeply flawed proposal is, of course, concerning. Fortunately, the national bodies recognized the problem before it was too late.
And perhaps it is also a clear signal that WG21's 'experts in the room' voting approach needs to be reconsidered.
