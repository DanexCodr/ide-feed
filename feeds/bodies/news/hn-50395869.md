Size It Up is the original daily visual size-estimation game by Magnitudle. Resize each target object until it looks correct beside a known reference, then lock in your estimate to reveal how close you were.

Every daily game has five comparisons. Each round is worth up to 100 points, for a maximum total score of 500.
