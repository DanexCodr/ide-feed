The Trump administration is trying to stop Utah from enforcing its gambling ban against Kalshi, making Utah the 10th state sued by the US government agency that regulates prediction markets.

Meanwhile, Utah Attorney General Derek Brown filed criminal charges in a state court against Kalshi and its founders on Wednesday. The attorney general’s office provided us with court filings listing charges against [KalshiEX, LLC](https://cdn.arstechnica.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Utah-v.-Kalshi.pdf), Kalshi co-founder and CEO [Tarek Mansour](https://cdn.arstechnica.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Utah-v.-Mansour.pdf), and Kalshi co-founder and COO [Luana Lopes Lara](https://cdn.arstechnica.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Utah-v.-Lara.pdf).

The company and two co-founders each face 13 felony charges and one misdemeanor for violating gambling laws. The charges are based on sports bets offered on Kalshi between September 13 and September 24. “The state did not issue arrest warrants for Monsour and Lopes Lara. Instead, they have been issued summons to respond to the charges in Third District Court in Salt Lake County,” The Salt Lake Tribune [wrote](https://www.sltrib.com/news/2026/10/28/utah-files-14-criminal-charges/).

The [Utah News Dispatch](https://utahnewsdispatch.com/2026/10/29/utah-criminal-charges-against-kalshi-gambling-ban/) quoted a Kalshi statement vowing to fight the charges. “This is a crazy overreach; Utah is clearly playing politics. We look forward to putting a stop to this in court, just like we did in Arizona,” the statement said, referring to [similar charges](https://utahnewsdispatch.com/2026/05/05/repub/arizonas-criminal-case-against-kalshi-permanently-blocked-by-federal-judge/) the company [beat](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.azd.1483385/gov.uscourts.azd.1483385.96.0.pdf) in Arizona in May.

Utah, which prohibits gambling, [filed a civil lawsuit against Kalshi](https://attorneygeneral.utah.gov/utah-sues-kalshi/) in a state court last week. Utah’s lawsuit seeks an order prohibiting Kalshi from offering bets on sports, elections, politics, and other events.

“Kalshi runs an online casino in Utah and calls it a prediction market. That violates Utah law,” Utah Attorney General Derek Brown, a Republican, said last week. “Not only do our state laws ban gambling but Utah is one of only two states where gambling is also prohibited under the state constitution. For 130 years, Utah has been clear: gambling has no place here, and we will enforce our laws.”

Utah already defeated Kalshi in federal court after the prediction market filed a preemptive lawsuit that sought to prevent the state from enforcing its gambling laws. Now that Utah is moving ahead in its attempt to shut down Kalshi, it must defend against a [lawsuit](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.utd.168095/gov.uscourts.utd.168095.1.0.pdf) filed by the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC).
