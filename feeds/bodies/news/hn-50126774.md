One of the keys to our success is bringing hard-working, talented technologists into the world of global logistics and giving them the opportunity to impact this massive, antiquated industry. There's tremendous opportunity in showing people that something that appears boring like global shipping is actually incredibly interesting. And there are few industries with a starker contrast between the complexity of the problem at hand and the sophistication of the companies attempting to solve it. Where competitors see cardboard boxes that need to get moved, and opportunities to buy low and sell high, we see a world of fascinating network economics, supply and demand elasticity questions, complex coordination challenges, routing optimization, and a huge array of other problems that are best solved with technology than any amount of industry experience. Don't get us wrong, we value domain experience and are blessed to have hired so many of the smartest people from global logistics to work on our team. But we absolutely require new approaches and innovation that almost always comes from outside the sector.
