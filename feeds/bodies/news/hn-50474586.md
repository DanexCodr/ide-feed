Hey guys. I recently watched the latency reports for my local voice assistant, which show a waveform plus silero probability for voice activity. I noticed silero lags visibly on the speech off signal.<p>There had to be a method to get this signal closer to what I felt is optimal. My idea was to use a 10 ms energy detector with a threshold that adapts to environment or room noise. Which worked amazingly well. It created more false positives but in my speculative generation pipeline that would not matter. If it&#x27;s wrong and the user continues speaking I can abort the pipeline fast.<p>I benchmarked my solution over ~3200 turns from my assistant logs and it came out ~110 ms faster initially. Still 100 ms now after some a fix which was needed for quiet speech.<p>I made it public if you want to have a look. If you measure it on your data I really appreciate any feedback. There&#x27;s a benchmark script in the repo.<p>Thx for reading, have a good day folks.
