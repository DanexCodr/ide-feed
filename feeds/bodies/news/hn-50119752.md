## Backups, domains and growth, handled

### Backups and recovery

- Scheduled backups run every day by default, at a quiet time for each service.
- Choose daily or weekly, and keep them for a week up to a year.
- Take a manual backup any time, for example before a big change.
- Restore in place, or into a brand-new service while the original keeps running.

### Domains

- Every app gets its own web address as soon as it is live.
- Add your own domain: we show the DNS record to create, then verify it.
- Certificates are issued and renewed for you.
- Pick which address is the primary one.

### Updates and growth

- Resize to a bigger server in the same region.
- Migrate to another region or plan; the app, data and domains move with it.
- Update the app to its latest version in one click.
- Change app settings from the dashboard; secrets stay masked.
