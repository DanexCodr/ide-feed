I was about to keep track of my clients and prospecting in Google Sheets for my solopreneur business, when I remembered that&#x27;s exactly what I advise my clients not to do.<p>Created Hermit CRM, an open source CRM for 1 person. Hermit helps to keep track of deals and prospecting. The platform is built for AI by storing the data in Markdown files in git, stored locally and can be accessed with a web app, CLI or MCP. No telemetry on the app. Building in public, even the website stats  are public as well.<p>It’s macOS first. Linux is less tested, and Windows isn’t supported.<p>Features I&#x27;m proud of: 
- import somebody from LinkedIn into to the CRM with 1 click
- adding a LinkedIn convo to the CRM with 1 click.
Both work without LinkedIn or CRM subs.<p>Don&#x27;t like what you see? Fork the Hermit with your AI.<p>Would love to learn how you&#x27;re using Hermit and where it doesn&#x27;t fit your workflows.
