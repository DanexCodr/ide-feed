Everything on the map is public and free to browse. An account is only needed to publish.

1. MeasureA RadiaCode detector in your pocket logs dose rate and location every second, on a walk, a road trip or a flight.![A hand holding a RadiaCode detector](https://radiaverse.com/landing/device-hand.webp)
2. Share a trackPublish the recording from the RadiaCode app. Your track joins the map, anonymously if you prefer, and its points feed the hexagon layer everyone sees.![A track of coloured measurement points on a city map](https://radiaverse.com/landing/track.webp)

Tracks, areas, comments and likes as they happen. The full feed follows the map in real time.

- Private until you publishTracks stay private by default. Making one public takes a separate, explicit consent.
- Publish anonymouslyPublic tracks can hide who recorded them while their measurements still count on the map. Your profile shows a nickname, never your Google or Apple name.
- A 24-hour delayPublic tracks are held back for a day before anyone else sees them, so nobody can follow you in real time.
- Export or delete, any timeRequest a copy of your data or delete your account from your profile. Accounts inactive for 36 months are removed, with a warning first.

- Precise positions onlyMeasurements with poor GPS accuracy, worse than 15 metres, are left off the map.
- Sensitive places protectedMeasurements inside protected locations are removed from public view.
- Reviewed areasNew areas and edits to existing ones are reviewed by moderators before they go public.
- Moderated conversationsComments pass automatic moderation checks, and signed-in users can report any track, area or comment.
- No raw coordinate dumpsPublic interfaces never hand out raw GPS tracks, and scraping is rate-limited and blocked.
