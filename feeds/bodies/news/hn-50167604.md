I study calculus with a pen tablet in xournal++. I kept screenshotting my work into Claude and reading long answers
back, when what I wanted was a teacher looking over my shoulder: circle the mistake, tick what's right, nudge me toward
the next step, and *show* me why. penmentor is that, one key away, on my own Claude subscription.

- Hints, not answers. A ladder from a nudge to naming the technique to a faint ghost of the next line: you do the thinking, which is what makes it stick.
- Feedback on your own work. The first real mistake circled where you made it, correct lines ticked, later lines that inherit the mistake underlined. The marks stay while you keep writing; Esc clears them.
- Practice that makes you recall. Questions on your key steps (answer by typing or by writing), find-the-error drills, "which technique fits?".
- See it. Box what you want to understand and get an interactive animation made with 3Blue1Brown's own manim: drag the bounds, retype a value, watch Riemann sums close in.
- Whole page aware. A small xournal++ plugin shares the page you are on, not just what fits on screen.

penmentor runs on the AI command-line agent you already use, on that subscription. Pick the model in *Settings*; its
prefix says which agent answers:

| Prefix | Agent | For example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `claude:` | [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) | `claude:opus`, `claude:sonnet`, `claude:haiku` |
| `codex:` | [Codex](https://github.com/openai/codex) | `codex:gpt-5.6-terra` |
| `agy:` | Antigravity CLI | `agy:gemini-4-argon-medium`, `agy:gemini-3.8-flash-medium` |

Settings lists the agents you have installed. How each one is run and what it sees: [the guide](https://github.com/AbdullahAlhariri/penmentor/blob/main/docs/guide.md#how-it-works-and-what-leaves-your-computer).

```
git clone https://github.com/AbdullahAlhariri/penmentor ~/src/penmentor
~/src/penmentor/bin/penmentor --selftest
```

Linux on X11 (best with i3), with one of those agents logged in (`claude`, `codex` or `agy`), plus `rofi`, `slop`,
`xdotool` and PyQt6. Bind `bin/penmentor` to a key ([example](https://github.com/AbdullahAlhariri/penmentor/blob/main/contrib/i3.conf)). Setup, keys and how it works: [the guide](https://github.com/AbdullahAlhariri/penmentor/blob/main/docs/guide.md).
