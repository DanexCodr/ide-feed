I&#x27;ve worked for a long time in the storage field during my career and ZFS is the piece of engineering I admire most in that space. I wanted a way to pay homage to it and to explore and better understand its internals. So I built Uberblok: a museum of ZFS. A deep dive into copy-on-write, txgs, vdevs, RAID-Z, snapshots, the ARC, metaslabs, the ZIL, send&#x2F;receive with explanations and animated diagrams.<p>I&#x27;m sure people here know these internals better than I do, so corrections are very welcome.<p>I built it with my agent swarm organization, Algotitan.<p>I hope you&#x27;ll enjoy the deep dive into Uberblok as much as I do.<p>Museum: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;uberblok.storagebits.workers.dev" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;uberblok.storagebits.workers.dev</a>
