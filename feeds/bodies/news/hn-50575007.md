I recently shared this map (above) showing the countries of the world as drawn by over 3,000 people playing the [**Borderline**](https://www.habibicode.org/borderline) country border game. This [**combined map**](https://www.habibicode.org/thedrawnworld) brings together all the borders and coastlines drawn by players in Borderline and its companion game, [**Coastlines**](https://www.habibicode.org/coastline).

In Borderline, players attempt to draw the outlines of individual countries on a blank map from memory. The results can be surprisingly interesting, which is why, alongside the global map above, you can also explore [**How the crowd draws every country**](https://www.habibicode.org/countries).

This multiple-map visualization animates between each country's actual borders and a composite outline created from all the players' attempts to draw it.
