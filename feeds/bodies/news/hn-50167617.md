I built a quiz game around TypeSafe&#x27;s new Jev model, which has vision capabilities. Jev looks at a picture and a question and picks an answer (or taps a place) in about a quarter of a second.<p>I&#x27;ve always loved quiz games and thought this would be a fun way to see what it can do and how fast it is. I made the cards from scratch, and Jev gets exactly the picture you see. More cards will come over time.<p>Play against Jev on your own (there&#x27;s a leaderboard for each mode), or start a live match and challenge your friends, with Jev as one more player.<p>The code is open source, so feel free to build your own game or make more cards that I can publish.<p>Play: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;jevcards.com" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;jevcards.com</a>
