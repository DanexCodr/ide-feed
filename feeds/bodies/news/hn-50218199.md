A soap film is a sheet of water between two layers of soap molecules, often thinner than a micrometer. A vertical film drains under gravity and is thinner at the top, so in white light it shows bands of color ordered by thickness. Newton watched them on bubbles kept under a glass cover ([*Opticks*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/33504), Book II, Part I, Observation 17): colored rings formed at the top, widened and moved down as the water drained, and a [black spot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soap_film#Black_spots) grew at the top before the bubble broke.

The color at thickness ddd follows from two ingredients: the fraction of light the film reflects at each wavelength λ\lambdaλ, and the three [color-matching functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIE_1931_color_space#Color_matching) that the [CIE](https://cie.co.at) (the International Commission on Illumination) uses to reduce a spectrum to a color. The first is a single cosine in 1/λ1/\lambda1/λ, so the color is a fixed white minus the [cosine transform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sine_and_cosine_transforms) of the color-matching functions, evaluated at 2nd2nd2nd, the extra optical path traveled by light reflected from the film’s back surface, where nnn is the refractive index of water. The computed colors agree with the table of thicknesses that Newton compiled for them.

Light is an electromagnetic wave: an electric field and a magnetic field that oscillate together as the wave travels. The **amplitude** of a beam is the size of its electric field’s oscillation, and the brightness of the beam is proportional to the square of its amplitude. ## The Michel-Lévy chart

A plate of a [birefringent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birefringence) crystal between crossed [polarizers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polarizer), at 45°45°45° to them, transmits the fraction sin⁡2(πΓ/λ)\sin^2(\pi\Gamma/\lambda)sin2(πΓ/λ) of the polarized light, where the **retardation** Γ\GammaΓ is the optical path difference between the plate’s two polarizations. That is R/4r2R/4r^2R/4r2 with Γ=τ\Gamma = \tauΓ=τ, so the strip in the widget is also the [interference color chart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interference_colour_chart) that mineralogists use to identify minerals in [thin section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thin_section), with 2nd2nd2nd as the retardation (for the same illuminant, and with the birefringence taken constant across the band).
