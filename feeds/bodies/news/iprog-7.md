Perhaps we don't have to wait for a bipedal robot to implement something like robocop for household security. Amazon seems to have the solution, and at a reasonable price. Ring. an Amazon-owned company, creates reasonably-priced [security gadgets](<https://link.amazon/B06mpi2OA>) like video cameras and doorbells. Now it has announced Ring Robots, with two products waiting in the wings. One is the Ring Drone Cam, a quadcopter-style drone for indoor use. The other is the [Ring Rover Cam](<https://link.amazon/B04Jy0Ztj>), a small security robot that can trundle around your house looking for problems. Both are powered by AI - what else. The Drone has a base station that charges it until an alert from another sensor sends it up to find out what the problem is. I'm sure they are right - at least at first. The buzz of a drone is a disturbing sound and, let's face, it who wants an encounter with a drone even if its propellers are caged. And noticing a camera coming towards you which is displaying your mugshot to some remote location is definitely deterrent. However, there are some issues that need to be thought about. The first is that all a burglar really has to do is to throw a blanket over either to neutralize them, making them little better than a static camera. The other issue is that many parts of the world have laws that prohibit setting traps for intruders. If an intruder trips over the Rover arguing it was driven at them, or if the Drone impacts the head of an intruder, it could make for an interesting court case.  On the other hand, I can also see makers adding, what shall we call them, improved deterrents. We have had drones with flame throwers and chainsaws and while this particular drone doesn't seem powerful enough to lift either that isn't going to stop the more creative among us inventing.
