The **AT&T UNIX PC**, aka PC 7300 aka 3B1, is a 1985 Unix desktop developed by Convergent Technologies and sold by AT&T. Emulating it is nothing new. Phil Pemberton started [FreeBee](https://github.com/philpem/freebee) in 2010, and Neozeed has covered it here since: [booting the diagnostic disk](https://virtuallyfun.com/2011/02/19/att-3b1-emulator/) in 2011, [booting AT&T UNIX](https://virtuallyfun.com/2014/05/21/philip-pembertons-3b1-emulator-now-boots-att-unix/) in 2014 thanks to Andrew Warkentin, and [running the C compiler](https://virtuallyfun.com/2020/08/29/thanks-to-shadyjesse-philpems-freebee-can-now-run-the-c-compiler/) in 2020 thanks to shadyjesse. Arnold Robbins added bigger disks and keeps the 3B1 software alive at [skeeve.com/3b1](https://www.skeeve.com/3b1/). However there was no networking, and the mouse had to be grabbed with F10.

Now it all has been patched up in my fork of FreeBee:

- Ethernet and TCP/IP! The 3B1 Ethernet board, borrowed from MAME (Visionik‘s 3B1 board and Patrick Mackinlay’s LANCE), on libslirp. Wollongong’s WIN/TCP does telnet, ftp and rsh, out to the Internet.
- Seamless mouse! No more F10 grab. The pointer just moves in and out of the window, and the scroll wheel works!
- Turbo and CPU idle! 10x as fast as a real 3B1 (1x to 32x), with the clock in real time. And an idle VM now takes about 4% of a Mac core instead of 44%.
- Fixes! No more frozen Office forms, stuck Alt key or flicker on macOS.
- Scripting! A control socket and an MCP server.

It’s all in a pull request to Phil’s upstream FreeBee, still open: [https://github.com/philpem/freebee/pull/57](https://github.com/philpem/freebee/pull/57), and the CPU fix in one to his [Musashi](https://github.com/philpem/Musashi/pull/1).

**The ultimate UNIX PC image!** UNIX System V 3.51m with Fix Disk 2.0 and 187 packages: nearly every program made for the UNIX PC, like Microsoft Word, Multiplan, dBASE III, WordStar 2000, GCC 2.5.8, GNU Emacs 19, TeX and over 80 games. It comes from THE STORE!, the 3B1 archive and the disk images of Arnold Robbins, Alain Knaff and the VCF. Credit is due!

Behold! UNIX System V 3.51m and the Office on an emulated AT&T UNIX PC:

As usual I have prepared a ready to run, portable image with FreeBee binaries for macOS (Apple Silicon), Linux (x64 and arm64) and Windows (x64). Just unpack and run `run.sh` (or `run.cmd` on Windows). At “Please login:” type tenox for the Office, or root for a shell. There are no passwords. For Archaic, start WRP on your computer first.
