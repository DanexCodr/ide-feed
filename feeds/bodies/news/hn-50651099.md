I was inspired by Mochi and Anki, but wanted to make something that’s a mix of the two. Anki is very confusing and has an outdated interface while Mochi is very simple and has a more minimalist interface. Olumino is the combination of both.<p>I also added Japanese flashcard support, including kana readings, for anyone learning the language.<p>I’m a CS student building this in my free time, so I’d love to hear what people think!
