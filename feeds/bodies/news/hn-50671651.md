## Create your graph

SessionGraph reads your harness' session store, indexes it and opens a desktop app for you to play around. Everything happens locally.

### Run it locally

Paste this into your terminal to open SessionGraph.

## A database for all your sessions

Search across your harnesses from one local index. Find an old conversation, pick up where you left off, or look up what changed.

“Find my sessions about invoice retries.”
