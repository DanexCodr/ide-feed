- What is LEGO® Daft Punk™ Concert?LEGO® Daft Punk™ Concert is an exciting product idea that brings the legendary musicians to life in brick form. Submitted by a fan on the crowdsourcing platform LEGO Ideas, this creation has been selected for product development. This means the Fan Designer RobotRock will collaborate with LEGO designers to create the final LEGO Ideas set, allowing fans to construct and display a LEGO version of the iconic duo.
