I&#x27;m glad to announce that I&#x27;m releasing my new book, Learning to Play Vim.<p>When I started using Vim more than a decade ago, I wished there was a manual out there teaching how to use this fantastic editor step by step, beginning with the foundations up to more advanced features. Of course at the time I did the vimtutor, but it was too dense for me to remember much from it. I also read the entire Vim manual (`:help manual`), but I didn&#x27;t like it: it was too detailed in places (for example `02.8 Finding help` completely lost me for a while), and other features didn&#x27;t get the treatment they deserved.<p>So I decided to write my own Vim manual. It took me more than 4 years and 2987 focused pomodoros (1244 hours) of work to finally get it done!<p>The result:<p>- 21 chapters showing the most important Vim features step by step.<p>- 170 exercises with solutions.<p>- Many real-life examples to show how Vim can be used and configured for your own needs.<p>- The related Vim&#x27;s help pages to dig further into each feature.<p>Most configs are both written in Vimscript and Lua for Neovim users. This book explores the common features between Vim and Neovim, so don&#x27;t expect anything about Neovim-specific features (like treesitter or LSP for example).<p>- The landing page: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;themouseless.dev&#x2F;vim&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;themouseless.dev&#x2F;vim&#x2F;</a><p>- A sample of the book with the entire table of contents and the first three chapters covering most of the basics: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;themouseless.dev&#x2F;content&#x2F;learning_to_play_vim_sample.pdf" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;themouseless.dev&#x2F;content&#x2F;learning_to_play_vim_sample...</a>.<p>I wrote this with my bare hands, no LLM involved to spoil the fun.
