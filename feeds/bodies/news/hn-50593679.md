About Tasklet

## Better models are only the beginning.

We believe every company will have AI employees. Not another chatbot to prompt or a workflow to babysit, but teammates with real responsibilities: closing the books, keeping a sales pipeline moving, helping customers, and following through on the work between messages. Tasklet is building the product that makes those employees possible.

Our vision is a team of virtual teammates that learn your business, collaborate with your people, and follow through around the clock. Customers already build custom agents, connect their business tools, and work with them in Tasklet, Slack, email, and text. We’re making it easier for an entire company to work with those agents—and for the people responsible to understand and control what they do.

The hard part is everything between a capable model and a colleague you can depend on. We combine models from multiple labs, swarms of parallel workers, cloud computers, and connections to business systems in one agent harness. Then we make that power understandable: what an agent owns, what it did, when it needs help, and what it is allowed to do next.

The AI labs are racing to build better models. We’re racing to turn that intelligence into a workforce businesses can actually rely on. We can use the best technology from any lab and focus our entire company on that problem. This is a chance to help define a new category of software—not add one more feature to the old one.
