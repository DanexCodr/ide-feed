# **Search** is a browser for the Mac with nothing in the way. **About ten megabytes**, the engine already in macOS, and a window that opens in **a third of a second**. Free, for any Mac on macOS 14 or later.

Version 1.0.14 · 10.8 MB · Free · [Intel Mac?](https://searchbrowser.app/download/intel)

It works: pick a tab, open a new one, swipe the tabs sideways for another space.

**Two ways to hold it.** Titles across the top, or down the side. The grey slides to the tab you pick either way, and a pinned site is one letter.

![The top-left corner of Search with the tab strip](https://searchbrowser.app/search/corner-strip.png)

***Tab strip.** The grey slides to the tab you pick.*

**Spaces.** Separate sets of tabs — work, home, a side project — each with its own icon. Two fingers sideways over the tabs and the next one slides in beside this one; past the last, a new one, signed in where you already are or starting afresh. [Try it in the window above.](https://searchbrowser.app/#app)

**Read, watch, and nothing else.** Reading mode keeps the article and its pictures, set to be read. Leave a video's tab and it follows you in a small window, above every app.

**Light enough to forget it's there.** Measured against Chrome and Safari on the same Mac, the same afternoon, the same pages. Medians of three runs; [the method and every run](https://searchbrowser.app/measured).

**10.8 MB on disk.** No engine of its own to carry.

**320 ms to the first window.** Launched cold, no page open.

**51 MB before the first page.** Every process the app owns.

**11 processes with five tabs open.** Where Chrome runs 29.

- **Pinned tabs.** A letter at the head of the row. ⌘W puts it to sleep; it never leaves.
- **Ads blocked before they load.** In the network layer — no scripts, no counter.
- **Hide anything.** ⌘⇧H, click a thing, it's gone from that site for good.
- **Reading mode.** The article, its pictures, nothing else. ⌘⇧R.
- **Floating video.** Leave YouTube or Netflix and the video follows, above every app.
- **Passwords in your keychain.** Shown with Touch ID. Passwords, bookmarks and history come over from Chrome or Dia in a click.
- **Spaces.** Separate sets of tabs, each with its icon. Two fingers sideways to switch; stay signed in, or start afresh.
- **Chrome extensions.** Straight from the Chrome Web Store, in a click. On macOS 15.4 or later.
- **A sidebar that steps aside.** Tabs down the side or across the top. ⌘S folds the column away; the left edge brings it back.
- **Private tabs.** ⌘⇧N. Its own cookies, no history, no place in tomorrow's session.
- **Web Inspector.** Inspect Element on any page, the console at ⌥⌘J — WebKit's own tools.
- **Open source.** The code is [on GitHub](https://github.com/driceroland/Search) — read it, build it yourself, or send a fix.
