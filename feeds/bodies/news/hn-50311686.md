This is an episode guessing game using Star Trek: The Next Generation (TNG) episode images as clues.

Your objective is to correctly identify the episode featured in the given images.

You have a maximum of six attempts at guessing.

If you select the correct season, even if your episode guess is incorrect, you'll receive feedback.

Strive to guess the episode with as few guesses as possible.

Each image clue is easier that the previous. Come back tomorrow for another game.

Enjoy!
