Current education system focuses on wrong things, and is subject to traditional practices that were developed due to need of scaling, instead of due to improvement of the system itself.

The core issue is that school is not actually focusing on brain development and "learning how to learn". By the way, this is what I was told as the reason of studying history and all those other subjects I didn't like. Instead it focuses on developing concrete skills in a bunch of different practices, and I will explain why it is completely wrong.

The brain develops when it is exposed to a new experience/task, because first brain is doing the task inefficiently using several existing neural centers, and then in around ~15 minutes it finds a neural center in between of those, and now performs the task much more efficiently.

It needs more studying, but at this point I am pretty sure that to become a genius at math, the best play is to learn everything except math for multiple years, to develop brainpower, and only then to go into math when your brain is ready, and reach the frontier much faster.

Instead the school system does completely opposite - the student can't choose what to study, needs to memorize concrete facts and crevices of systems (the grades measure that instead of measuring how well the student was exposed to various experiences). As provider of education the school should be graded on how well it provides the service of education, instead of users being graded on how well they consume the service.
