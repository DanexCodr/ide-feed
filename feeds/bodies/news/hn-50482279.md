After my last trash clock (<a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;news.ycombinator.com&#x2F;item?id=49930439">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;news.ycombinator.com&#x2F;item?id=49930439</a>), I wanted to see how low I could go. My latest clock is built mainly from empty drink cartons, plus a few skewers, some paper straws, a piece of string, some nuts as weights, and lots of hot glue. It has a pendulum, an escapement, and hands for seconds, minutes, and hours, and runs for about an hour.<p>The design of the gears and escapement was AI-assisted: Claude built me interactive browser tools that generate templates and 3D-printable jigs and tools.<p>I’m happy to answer your questions!
