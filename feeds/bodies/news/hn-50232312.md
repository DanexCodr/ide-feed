I was playing around with visualising the S&amp;P 500 by market cap and decided to add employees to each company&#x27;s bubble.<p>What surprised me was how empty some of the biggest companies looked.<p>Nvidia is worth $5.2 trillion but employs only 42,000 people. That&#x27;s around $124 million in market cap per employee. Meanwhile, Accenture employs 814,000 people and is worth $146 billion.<p>Each stick figure represents 10,000 employees.
