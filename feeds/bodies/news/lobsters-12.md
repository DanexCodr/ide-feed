Motif Central brings together information about **Motif, Xt, Xlib and X11**:
how they fit together, how to program with them, and where to find the
manuals, examples and historical material that explain them.

Begin with [the X programming stack](https://motif-central.org/x-stack/), build
[your first Motif application](https://motif-central.org/getting-started/), or explore the
[programming resources](https://motif-central.org/programming/). The history pages describe the
libraries' origins, while the [archive guide](https://motif-central.org/archives/) helps you navigate
older documentation.
