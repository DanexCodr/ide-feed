Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS is here after nearly a year of thoughtful feature development.    

With tons of improvements, new features, and the usual bug fixes, Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS feels right at home from the first boot. And when you're ready to make it your own, you can customize it until it looks and feels exactly the way you think a desktop should.

It all starts with Constellations.

## **What are Constellations?**

Constellations are COSMIC’s all-in-one bundled themes.  With a single click you can change the wallpaper, accent colors, icons, and fonts. It's literally a one-click switch for a full aesthetic change of your desktop. Instead of having to go to Desktop > Wallpaper, then Desktop > Appearance, then Desktop Panel, then Desktop Dock, then Desktop Window Management, all you do is go to Settings > Desktop > Appearance, select between dark or light mode, enable or disable automatic mode switching, and then select your theme.

You can also apply the Frosted Glass effect to a Constellation, and edit nearly every aspect of a Constellation. Once you have your Constellation exactly as you want, export it so you have it as a backup, or to share with others.

With Constellations, the desktop is truly yours to customize and keep.

## **Also in 26.04:**

### **Drawing tablet support**

Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS adds drawing tablet support, which brings precise, natural pen input and unlocks features like tilt sensitivity. That opens the door for graphic design, photo editing, animation, note-taking, CAD, and GIS applications.

This release also adds an on-screen keyboard with touch and gamepad input.

### **Automatic updates**

You can now enable automatic updates during the initial setup or within Settings > System & accounts > System & application updates. No missing crucial updates to the OS!
