🔎 Finally [solved the mystery](https://x.com/perishable/status/2123272927252738098). At some point this year (around a few months ago), my bandwidth/data usage went thru the roof. Figured out it was Chrome’s recent AI features. Then got immediate bandwidth-relief after disabling all the “AI innovations” and “On-device” stuff. Here is a screenshot of the settings:

I simply disabled everything on that settings page, and the needless bandwidth leakage **stopped immediately**.

Don’t take my word for it. Fire up your favorite activity/network monitor. Watch the network activity while Chrome’s AI features are enabled, which they are **by default**. Note the continual bandwidth/data spend coming from Chrome, which if you care to monitor long-term adds up to *hundreds* of GBs per month (depending on setup, usage, etc.).

After watching the meter spin for a while, go into the Chrome settings and disable all the “AI innovations” and “On-device” stuff. Then return to the network monitor and watch it virtually flatline. **Problem solved**.
