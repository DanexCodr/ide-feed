The PHP security team would like to give advance notice of an
        **upcoming security release**.

**It is scheduled for Wednesday, 21 October 2026 at 14:00 UTC.**

It addresses two critical vulnerabilities, both rated CVSS v4.0 9.2.

        soon as the release is available.

        vulnerability become public, or should evidence of active exploitation

        published with the release.
