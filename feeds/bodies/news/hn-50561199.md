I want to show you HippoMaps. It is a fully featured map and navigation app built on OpenStreetMap with a custom Overpass API engine for really fast search of POIs, areas and ways.<p>My aim with HippoMaps is to get to a level where I don&#x27;t need Google Maps anymore. The biggest hurdle IMHO with OSM based apps is the lack of nice and fast search (where GMaps excels at). To get there I started working on a deterministic (as in no LLMs used) parser of queries that translates them to the Overpass QL.<p>Search queries are split into tokens and the algorithm tries to guess &quot;what&quot; you want to search and &quot;where&quot;. The former comes from a word list (known brands, sports, OSM tags) and the latter from the geocoder service (Photon).<p>You can get quite creative like &quot;cafes in Zurich by the Limmat&quot;, &quot;peaks over 4000 m in Valais&quot;, &quot;pharmacies open now in Bern&quot;. It&#x27;s good for discovering hiking trails, e.g. &quot;hiking trails from Arth-Goldau to Rigi Kulm&quot;. It knows how to find stuff between two places, next to a place, near a place and so on. You can stack those as well like &quot;bridges over the danube in serbia 50 km from novi sad&quot;.<p>Except the search it has almost all of the features you would expect from a map like turn-by-turn navigation (Valhalla) with voice (Piper), public transport navigation in Europe (MOTIS), live traffic (only a few countries so far).<p>The app is GPL-3.0: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;cdutsov&#x2F;hippomaps" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;cdutsov&#x2F;hippomaps</a><p>I&#x27;m looking forward to feedback on searches that work or don&#x27;t work for you + overall comments.
