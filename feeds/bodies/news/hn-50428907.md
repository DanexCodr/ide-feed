hey HN, i built this because i wanted to know what had actually photographed my street.<p>type in any place and it finds every satellite that passed over it in the last 7 days (SGP4 on the CelesTrak catalog, runs in your browser in about half a second), then pulls the real Sentinel-2 &#x2F; Landsat &#x2F; NASA images from those passes.<p>some fun bits:
- the pass search can&#x27;t skip a pass. checked it against a brute force on poles, the antimeridian and tiny swaths: 0 missed
- all ~17k objects in orbit are drawn on the GPU, solving Kepler&#x27;s equation every frame
- Sentinel-2 timestamps are often 5-10 min off from when the sat actually crosses your spot, which was a fun one to figure out<p>it only says &quot;watched&quot; if a real image exists, and it doesn&#x27;t store your location.<p>code: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;dev-Armaan&#x2F;satellites" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;dev-Armaan&#x2F;satellites</a><p>would love feedback, especially on what data sources to add
