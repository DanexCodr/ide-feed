Hi HN, I made a small macOS utility for my own iOS simulator workflow.<p>I kept running into two annoyances: getting config files in and out of a simulator is clunky (digging into CoreSimulator UUID folders or running simctl), and on the iPhone Duo simulator I couldn&#x27;t even drag and drop images onto the window at all.<p>SimDrop puts every running iOS simulator in Finder&#x27;s sidebar (under Locations) with two folders:<p>- Downloads&#x2F; (Files app &gt; On My iPhone &gt; Downloads)
- Photos and Videos&#x2F; (the simulator&#x27;s Photos library)<p>Simulators appear when they boot and disappear when they shut down. Everything is also reachable from the terminal under ~&#x2F;Library&#x2F;CloudStorage&#x2F;SimDrop-SimDrop&#x2F;.<p>A couple of caveats up front:
- Dragging moves files by default (same as iCloud Drive, because it uses File Provider on the startup volume)—hold Option to copy.
- macOS 15+ and iOS simulators only (not physical devices).<p>It&#x27;s free, open source (GPL-3.0), signed and notarized, and installable via Homebrew:<p><pre><code>  brew install --cask rxliuli&#x2F;tap&#x2F;sim-drop
</code></pre>
Just sharing in case others run into the same friction—feedback or PRs welcome!
