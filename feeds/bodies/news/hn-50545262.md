Teskooano (tes-koo-ah-no) puts our Solar System and endless generated systems in your browser, with every body pulling on every other. Fly from orbit down to the ground of any solid world, land, and drive a buggy across it. Watch light bend around black holes, or take the guided Grand Tour.

### [Sol](https://teskooano.space/explore/c/sol/)

An ultracool dwarf with seven rocky planets in a resonant chain, three of them in its habitable zone.

The first transiting circumbinary planet: a Saturn-mass world orbiting a K and an M dwarf every 229 days.

Fiction, from Firefly: the White Sun and the Core, with Georgia, Red Sun, Kalidasa and Blue Sun circling it, from Londinium and Persephone out to Shadow and Miranda.

### [Kerbol](https://teskooano.space/explore/c/kerbol/)

From Kerbal Space Program: Kerbol and its worlds, from Moho to Eeloo, on the game's own orbits, with Jool's moons made light enough to survive real gravity.

### [Alpha Centauri](https://teskooano.space/explore/c/alpha-centauri/)

The nearest stars: Alpha Centauri A and B on an 80-year orbit, and Proxima Centauri 8,700 AU out with two planets.

### [HD 189733](https://teskooano.space/explore/c/hd-189733/)

A K dwarf with the transiting hot Jupiter HD 189733 b, the first exoplanet whose colour and winds were measured.

### Generate your own

Any seed makes a new system: single and binary stars, neutron stars, black holes, planets, moons, rings and comets, under a sky of its own. The same seed always makes the same system.
