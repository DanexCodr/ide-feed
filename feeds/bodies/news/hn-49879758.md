Kindle Comic Converter optimizes black &amp; white (or color) comics and manga for E-ink ereaders like Kindle, Kobo, ReMarkable, and more. Pages display in fullscreen without margins, with proper fixed layout support. Output works great in KOReader.<p>Full details on what KCC does is in the readme with plenty of photos!<p>KCC has been under continuous development since 2012 and has code sign on both Windows and macOS. Also available on Linux.<p>-the current kcc dev
