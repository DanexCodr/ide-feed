## The training objective

We start with [gemma-4-26B-A4B-it](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-26B-A4B-it). The goal is to spend fewer tokens getting to the answer, and change nothing else: preserve the knowledge, reasoning ability, answer quality, instruction following and safety behaviour.

## Evaluation methodology

Reasoning models are noisy to evaluate. At the recommended temperature of 1.0 both the content
and the length of a response move run to run, so a single seed tells you very little. Everything
below is multiple seeds per benchmark, with accuracy reported as the mean and a 95% confidence
interval across seeds.

**Both sides are the same run.** The original model and ours were evaluated by the same harness,
with identical sampling and serving settings. The original model was measured by us, not quoted
from published figures. The configuration:

- Harness — our internal evaluation suite, running both models on vLLM.
- Context — generation capped at 65,536 tokens per response on every benchmark, and per turn on τ²-bench.
- Sampling — thinking on; temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 64. Ours inherits Google's recommended preset unchanged, so no part of the comparison is a decoding change.
- Accuracy — the mean across seeds with a 95% t-interval, which says how far the answer moves when only the sampling seed changes.
- Seeds — 64 on AIME 2026, HMMT Nov 2025 and HMMT Feb 2026; 32 on GPQA-Diamond and LiveCodeBench; 16 on RealWorldQA, IFBench and τ²-bench; 8 on AA-LCR and LongBench v2; 4 on MMLU-Pro and MMMLU, whose question counts are large enough that question sampling is not the limiting noise.
- Question sets — AIME 2026 (30 problems), HMMT Nov 2025 (30), HMMT Feb 2026 (33), LiveCodeBench (175), GPQA-Diamond (198), MMLU-Pro (12,032), MMMLU (9,996), AA-LCR (100), LongBench v2 (370), RealWorldQA (765), IFBench (300) and τ²-bench (278 tasks across airline, retail and telecom).
- Scoring — exact match on the extracted answer, except LiveCodeBench, where the generated code is run against the problem's tests; IFBench, scored by its programmatic verifiers; and τ²-bench and AA-LCR, which are judged. τ²-bench also runs against a simulated user and is reported as the unweighted mean over its three domains.

### What the columns mean

- Accuracy (base) / Accuracy (ours) — as above; Δ accuracy is the difference between them, in percentage points.
- Δ mean thinking — the headline: the change in thinking tokens, totalled across the benchmark and divided by its question count, so a long question weighs more than a short one.
- Mean thinking tokens are the tokens before the reasoning closes, averaged over every question and seed of the benchmark, written base → ours.
- Truncated % — the share of responses whose thinking never closed, which score zero. Looping % — the share caught by a compression-based loop detector, which flags a response whose text compresses far beyond what ordinary prose does.
