## The Unbalanced Group Theorem

Hexagon 2610.00575 · 35 pages

A new group theory result that came out of this formalization project is reported in this paper. While formalizing the first-generation proof, we found counterexamples to a closure assertion used in a published route to the Unbalanced Group Theorem. The paper explains the obstruction and gives an alternative argument for the simple-group case, built on Solomon’s 1976 theory of maximal 2-components, whose key step is Theorem 2.3, the preservation of simple tops.

# The Classification of Finite Simple Groups, formalized in Lean

The classification of finite simple groups is one of the great collective achievements of mathematics. Its first-generation proof spans thousands of journal pages, written by about a hundred mathematicians over several decades. It shows that every finite simple group is cyclic of prime order, alternating, of Lie type, or one of 26 sporadic groups.

Here we present a complete formalization in Lean following the first-generation proof, with every logical step checked by Lean’s kernel. Along the way, we found an issue in a published argument for the Unbalanced Group Theorem. [The accompanying paper](https://cfsg.axiommath.ai/papers) explains the issue and the alternative route used in our proof. [Our blog post](https://axiommath.ai/research/the-enormous-theorem-formalized/) tells the full story.
