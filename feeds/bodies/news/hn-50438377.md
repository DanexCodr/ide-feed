## What is this?

Welcome, traveller. You have just discovered **FakeDOS**, a macOS screen saver that simulates a pixel-perfect DOS machine. And more.

Leave your Mac alone for a few minutes and something beautiful is going to happen: the screen fills with the warm 80×25 glow of a VGA text mode, an AWARD BIOS POST clicks through memory, and a friendly blinking **C:\>** prompt appears.

### Perfect for

- A conference room that does not need Slack popping up
- Pretending to be a 12-year-old running Landmark Test
- Tricking a co-worker into thinking you have lost it
- Actually getting people to leave you alone

## Features

### Five boot modes

### The Ghost Writer

Leave the prompt idle and a ghost starts typing commands by itself. It alternates between operating systems in an endless loop of simulated productivity.

- Many DOS commands, boot linux, some Linux commands, boot dos, repeat
- Realistic typing speed with human-like typos
- Press any key to take over and the ghost vanishes

### Landmark speed test

Type <kbd>FDOS</kbd> or <kbd>LANDMARK</kbd> at the prompt to see how fast your imaginary Pentium thinks it is.

Pure nostalgia, zero benchmarking.

## This is not a real DOS.

Absolutely nothing useful happens here.

FakeDOS is a pure simulation. Every single command you type produces carefully crafted fake output. No files are read, written, moved or deleted. No network connections are made. No processes are started. No disks are formatted.

When you type <kbd>FORMAT C:</kbd>, FakeDOS shows a terrifyingly realistic format sequence, complete with percentage counter and head/cylinder display, but your actual drive is perfectly untouched. When you type <kbd>DEL *.*</kbd>, nothing gets deleted. Ever.

It simulates. It pretends. It lies convincingly.  
But it never, ever touches your real system.

Not a terminal. Not an emulator. Not connected to the Pentagon. Probably.

## Installation

1. Drag **FakeDOS.app** into */Applications*.
2. Double-click to launch. It installs itself as a menu-bar icon.
3. Click the menu-bar icon to configure or start manually.
4. After the configured idle time (default: 5 min), FakeDOS takes over fullscreen.
5. Type <kbd>EXIT</kbd> to return to your real desktop.

<kbd>Cmd + ,</kbd> opens six pages of pure control: idle time, warp speed, the ghost writer, sound and more. The PC speaker can be silenced. Your co-workers cannot.
