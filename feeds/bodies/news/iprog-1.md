We take a look at a course about AI Agents, from the fundamentals of building a harness, to evaluation, training using reinforcement learning, and other advanced topics. Rather than focusing on single-step prompting, the course examines how agents interact with real environments, such as calling tools, navigating interfaces, writing code and collaborating with people,to complete complex tasks. It balances hands on systems implementation (engineering the "harness" around the model) with model training methods (such as supervised fine tuning and reinforcement learning) and active research questions. **Key topics covered:** Introduction and agent capabilities: Core agent loops, tool use, long context management, memory and skills and planning, task decomposition and multi agent coordination. Agent domains: Coding and software development agents, computer use agents (GUI and web navigation) and deep research agents. Training methods: Supervised Fine Tuning (SFT), Reinforcement Learning (RL) basics, advanced RL algorithms and scalable RL systems. Frameworks and safety: Building with frameworks like OpenHands, orchestration, observability and monitoring, sandboxing, credentials and agentic safety. Interaction and search: Multi agent interaction, human agent collaboration, the future of work, reranking/critic models and tree search **Course structure and prerequisites:** Hands on assignments: Three individual implementation assignments covering building an agent harness, designing evaluations for multi step tasks and training an agent using RL. Research project: A group based mini research project (teams of 2–3) in the second half of the semester, ending with poster presentations. Lecture highlights: Short student reflections on key takeaways from each lecture. Prerequisites: Prior serious experience training language models (e.g., through NLP/LM courses or equivalent industry experience). The good news is that you need not be a CMU student to enroll, since the lectures have been recorded and are up on Youtube for everyone to enjoy. Do not skip this first class opportunity to acquire hands on skills in building, evaluating and RL training AI agents alongside a compute supported research project. Link below.
