Hi everyone, we love maps and doing some fun side quests exploring this domain.<p>We were always surprised by the fact that products like ChatGpt recommends you  places to go which have been shut down for years.<p>Last March we built a benchmark measuring how often LLMs make mistakes when answering places-based queries (e.g. Recommend me a restaurant in Marina for a dinner). Even with the web grounding (which is a very expensive tool relative to the token price), the results were not good - 1 out 10 places in the best model were made up or shut-down.<p>When personal assistants started to come up, we were curious to check how well they do with those queries. It turns out that much better, but they also take longer time to answer. We make it slightly more challenging, we made a benchmark a bit more sophisticated and measured it.<p>The best one turned out to be Dots from OpenAI, and it is also one of the fastest.   The worst one was Grok Bot from XAI. We also measured Ask Maps from Google, but it was out of competition, because it is not technically a personal assistant. The more detailed results are available in the repository
