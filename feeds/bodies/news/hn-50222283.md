OpenAI’s math write-ups at [https://github.com/openai/math](https://github.com/openai/math) can be difficult to understand and can have incomplete citations.

[https://github.com/mathcommunity/oai-math-revised/](https://github.com/mathcommunity/oai-math-revised/ )seeks to be an inclusive effort to improve these write-ups, and the broader math community–students, postdocs, professors, and enthusiasts–is invited to participate.

The repo is browsable at [https://mathcommunity.github.io/oai-math-revised/](https://mathcommunity.github.io/oai-math-revised/)

We also invite the community to email [mathcommunityadmin@gmail.com](mailto:mathcommunityadmin@gmail.com) if they want to serve as admins/reviewers and for which categories from [https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/overview.pdf](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/overview.pdf) they’d be appropriate for reviews.
