We’ve made the difficult decision to wind down Val Town.

You took a big leap of faith to build on us. We thank you for your trust, and we’re sorry we can’t continue to serve you.

The bad news about our business – that we couldn’t compete in the cloud computing space – is good news for you migrating off: there are many wonderful hosting providers you can migrate to, and today’s LLMs are great at these types of migrations. We have [guides for migrating to Cloudflare, Vercel, Netlify, and Render.](https://docs.val.town/migration) Please reach out if you need help at [support@val.town](mailto:support@val.town) or on Discord.

We are pausing billing today, and will continue servicing existing subscriptions for free. If you pre-paid for our annual plan, we will issue you a partial refund.

Here’s the timeline on our wind-down:

| Service | End of life date |
| --- | --- |
| New signups and plan upgrades | Nov 10, 2026 (today) |
| Read-only mode (not including sqlite and blob storage) | Dec 10, 2026 |
| All other services | Jan 10, 2027 |
| Open-sourcing Val Town | Feb 1, 2027 |
