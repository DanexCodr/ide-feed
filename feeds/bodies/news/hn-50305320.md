Hi everyone!<p>I had the idea for catcopter on my mind for a long time (got the domain ~13 years ago). It&#x27;s a web multiplayer game where you can fly a dangerous cat and engage in competition with other players and critters (pterodactyls, chickens and angry penguins). I made it initially for me and my friends but it was quite fun so I thought I&#x27;ll share it here too.<p>The game is intentionally dorky and it was fun to develop. The tools I used were mostly Opus 5.5 (objects &#x2F; rigging &#x2F; motion), Tripo3d for some elements I struggled with and Fable for optimisation (cpu &#x2F; networking). Backend physics are done with Rapier. Luckily I had a lot of elements &#x2F; game dynamics done before from another project of mine (<a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;flat.social" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;flat.social</a>) so the actual iteration here took me around a week.
