Even for a tech-savvy consumer, it can be hard to understand the relationship between your vehicle, the data it generates, and your privacy and rights. With our connected vehicle and app user communities growing every day, Ford takes its responsibility in safeguarding customer data seriously. That’s why, today, Ford is announcing the Privacy Promise.

Ford is launching the Privacy Promise to make an explicit commitment to customers that their vehicle data is safe with us and they’re in control of what they share. Our newly launched Privacy Promise website provides education on our privacy practices and how to customize your vehicle data and privacy settings anytime in-vehicle or via the Ford App.

### Our Privacy Promise

We know that trust is earned. Protecting your personal information is one way we intend to earn it every day. Learn about our privacy practices by reading our Privacy Notices.

### Research Insights

As we created the Privacy Promise, Ford wanted to learn more about what customers understood about vehicle and data privacy.

We surveyed just over 1,000 U.S. consumers — both Ford and non-Ford owners. Our customers tell us that vehicle privacy is important to them.

As much as they value privacy, we also learned that customers understand the value of data sharing. A value our survey respondents kept returning to was control: the ability to adjust their settings on demand and in detail.

Ford created the Privacy Promise to help drive awareness of a customer’s responsibility and choices when it comes to their data.

Survey respondents listed automakers as more trusted stewards of privacy than many other large organizations that handle data.

Ford customers, in particular, showed more comfort than non-Ford customers with tech-forward, connected solutions like smart home devices.
