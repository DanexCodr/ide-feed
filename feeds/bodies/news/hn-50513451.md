I know that many folks here post their personal emails in the Who&#x27;s for Hire monthly pages and other places, and want to remind you that this public site gets views from folks with ill intent (or their bots&#x2F;agents) as well. I use unique emails for every site, so I immediately knew that the late AWS payments email sent to my HN email were fake. There were other tells, but I want to remind everyone to be vigilant! HN is public but even social media sites where you supposedly can make your page private I&#x27;ve had some of those emails suddenly have spam sent to as well. Please be safe everyone!<p>My favorite tells that an email is a scam:
- Before opening a surprising&#x2F;suspicious email, hover over the sender name to see the domain the sender came from (don&#x27;t open the email if it&#x27;s strange).
- _If_ you already opened the email, before clicking any links in the email, hover over them to see the domain that the link sends you to.<p>There are dozens more tips but a full article would be required, and these two will prevent you from falling for nearly all of them.
