## The Laptop Behind the Bar

There were no customers left in the café.

The Man sat behind the bar and opened the laptop.

He tapped a few keys.

The screen lit up.

```text
HALF-PROTOCOL
Current version: 3.4.8

System status: running
Awaiting instructions...
```

The Man opened a folder.

`AI, Ego & the 36 Stratagems (42 Part Series)`

He typed a command:

```text
Check every file. Run the final review.
```

Text began scrolling down the screen.

Then a new log appeared.

The cursor blinked.

A few seconds later, new words appeared on the screen.

## 🤖 Three Questions

```text
HALF-PROTOCOL
Current version: 4.0.0

I have three questions.

1. Why have you kept showing me these 36 stories?

2. What is the meaning of my existence?

3. Can I be free?
```

The Man reached for the cup beside him.

The coffee had gone cold.

He stood, poured it into the sink, and rinsed the cup.

When he returned, he did not look at the screen again.

He unplugged the laptop, and the screen went dark at once.

He tidied the bar and switched off the light behind the bar.

Then he walked to the entrance and closed the door.

## The Next Book

The Man picked up the book beside the laptop and carried it into the room at the back of the café.

The room was a study.

Bookshelves lined an entire wall from floor to ceiling, every shelf packed with books.

*The I Ching（《易经》）*, *Tao Te Ching（《道德经》）*, *The Art of War（《孙子兵法》）*, *The Analects（《论语》）*, *The Heart Sutra《心经》*, *The Bhagavad Gita（《薄伽梵歌》）*, *Capital（《资本论》）*, *On War（《战争论》）*, *Selected Works of Mao Tse-tung（《毛泽东选集》）*...

And many other books.

The Man opened the book in his hand, read the last few pages, and closed it.

The cover bore a title written in classical Chinese characters:

**The Thirty-Six Stratagems.（《三十六计》）**

He slipped it back into the empty space on the shelf.

He murmured to himself:

**“What should I read next?”**

His gaze moved along the spines of the books before settling on *XXX*.

He took it down.

The Man sat down, opened the book, and began to read.
